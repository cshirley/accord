* **Update**: Agent prompts `review-test.md`, `review-code.md`, `phase-test.md` (under `packages/accord-assets/agents/accord/`), changed to reduce adversarial loop rounds. [Agents](/architecture/agents.md) and [Crucible verification](/architecture/crucible-verification.md) unchanged. They don't describe prompt-level rules. No schema change: `Done when:` goes inside `recommendation`.
